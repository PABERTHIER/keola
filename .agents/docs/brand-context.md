# Keola — Brand Context

## 1. Brand in one sentence

**A warm, playful and slightly magical red-panda VTuber sanctuary: calm enough to feel comforting, lively enough to feel fun, and purposeful enough to connect entertainment with red-panda conservation.**

## 2. Identity

### Confirmed

Public references describe Keola Kumaneko as a **red-panda VTuber**, associated with the community name **Les Petits Esprits** and a strong conservation mission around red pandas.
Her public site links streaming and social platforms and includes sections for presentation, gallery, planning, credits, archives and partners.

The current site also foregrounds Red Panda Network, Safebear and sponsor/community information.

### Brand themes

1. **Red panda**
   - orange/russet fur
   - soft cream
   - warm brown
   - expressive eyes
   - cute but not childish

2. **Spirit / magic**
   - little spirits
   - glowing accents
   - firefly / wisp imagery
   - subtle blue/purple magical light
   - stars, sparkles and tiny floating particles

3. **Sanctuary**
   - forest
   - nature
   - cozy refuge
   - community
   - protection
   - calm

4. **Streaming / gaming**
   - playful cards
   - schedules
   - social links
   - video/media
   - event moments

5. **Altruism**
   - conservation
   - charity
   - community support
   - friendly calls to action

## 3. Personality model

The visual personality should sit between:

- cozy ↔ energetic
- cute ↔ professional
- magical ↔ contemporary
- playful ↔ calm

Avoid moving too far toward either extreme.

### Desired personality

- warm
- welcoming
- expressive
- gentle
- mischievous in small doses
- modern
- community-oriented
- caring
- optimistic
- slightly whimsical

### Avoid

- generic gamer aesthetics
- aggressive neon cyberpunk
- excessive kawaii overload
- childish UI
- dark/heavy horror styling
- corporate minimalism
- luxury fashion minimalism
- visual clutter
- excessive gradients
- too many decorative illustrations competing with content

## 4. Audience feeling

A first-time visitor should feel:

> “This is a cozy little world I want to explore.”

A returning community member should feel:

> “This is home.”

A sponsor/partner should feel:

> “This is a polished, credible creator brand.”

A conservation-minded visitor should feel:

> “There is a real cause behind the character.”

## 5. Visual storytelling

The site should feel like entering a **small magical sanctuary**, not entering a conventional portfolio.

The character is the emotional anchor.

The environment is supportive:

- warm paper-like surfaces
- soft natural textures
- restrained foliage
- tiny spirit particles
- rounded shapes
- hand-drawn micro-details

The interface remains contemporary underneath.

## 6. Brand motifs

Recommended recurring motifs:

- red-panda tail stripes
- tiny paw prints
- leaves
- acorns
- apples
- stars
- fireflies
- spirit wisps
- small hearts
- rounded clouds
- little woodland objects

Use these as **micro-decoration**, not as a wallpaper.

## 7. Logo / wordmark direction

If a logo is available, preserve it.

For surrounding typography:

- use a distinctive friendly display face for large headings
- use a highly legible neutral sans-serif for body copy
- optionally use a handwritten accent only for small labels or playful annotations

Do not make the whole interface handwritten.

## 8. Photography / artwork

Artwork should be treated as a primary brand asset.

Preferred treatment:

- rounded clipping
- organic masks
- subtle paper/card framing
- occasional sticker-like overlap
- generous whitespace around character art
- natural warm backgrounds

Avoid:

- hard rectangular image grids everywhere
- generic stock-photo styling
- overly glossy UI frames

## 9. Original-site evidence and Nuxt-site strategy

The original `keola.tv` site exposed a broad amount of content and many external destinations.
The current Nuxt site preserves that richness while improving hierarchy.
Check its pages and `app/data/site.ts` before treating the suggestions below as current functionality.

### Keep

- social ecosystem
- presentation
- gallery
- schedule
- credits
- archives
- partners
- community/conservation
- media kit

### Improve

- stronger homepage story
- clear primary CTA
- clearer hierarchy
- better visual grouping
- more breathing room
- stronger mobile navigation
- more polished typography
- consistent cards
- stronger art direction
- better distinction between primary and secondary information

## 10. Core design principle

**Cute details, serious foundation.**

The interface should be polished and professional first; the fun comes from illustration, micro-interactions, motifs and small moments of surprise.
